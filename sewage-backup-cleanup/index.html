Why Does My Home Smell Like Sewage When Drains Run Slow?

A slow tub drain, gurgling toilet or a floor drain that bubbles can signal a blocked sewer line. If wastewater is rising in a tub, shower or basement floor drain, the line is backing up into the house and it is time to stop using water.

Backups come from clogged laterals, tree roots, grease or a problem at the main. A plumber clears the line and finds the cause. Our work begins once the flow stops and the contaminated area needs safe cleanup.

Protect yourself first

Stop using water in the house, including toilets, sinks and washing machines. Keep children, pets and anyone with a weakened immune system out of the area.

Do not clean sewage with household tools or a standard wet vac. Wastewater contains bacteria and should be handled with protective gear.

How Sewage Cleanup Differs

Sewage water is categorized as the most contaminated type of water, so the work follows a stricter process than a clean leak. Crews wear protective suits, gloves and respirators, seal off the area and extract the water with equipment that is cleaned afterward.

Porous materials that touched sewage, such as carpet, pad, insulation and drywall, are removed and disposed of rather than dried. Hard surfaces are cleaned and disinfected. The structure is then dried so no moisture remains behind.

Contain and protect

The area is isolated and crews put on protective gear.

Extract

Wastewater is removed with equipment dedicated to contaminated water.

Remove porous materials

Carpet, pad and drywall touched by sewage are cut out and bagged.

Sanitize and dry

Hard surfaces are cleaned and treated, then the space is dried.

What to Do Before Help Arrives

  • Stop using water, including toilets and sinks.
  • Keep children, pets and anyone ill out of the area.
  • Do not clean sewage with household tools.
  • Wash hands thoroughly if you touched the water.

Insurance and Sewage Backups

Sewer backup coverage is often a separate endorsement and not part of a standard homeowners policy. Check your policy for sewer or drain backup coverage, and photograph the area before cleanup. Ask your carrier about the plumber invoice, which can help show the cause.

Sewer Lines and Douglasville Properties

The Douglasville-Douglas County Water and Sewer Authority maintains the public sewer system. If your backup started at the property line, ask whether the blockage is in the public main or in your private lateral, since that decides who is responsible for the repair.

Where mature trees stand near a lateral, roots can enter older clay or loose joints in the line. A camera inspection after a clearing shows whether roots or a collapse caused it.

Related Services

After sanitizing, structural drying clears moisture from the structure. If floodwater mixed with the sewage, see flood damage restoration, and for basement backups, basement water damage cleanup.
