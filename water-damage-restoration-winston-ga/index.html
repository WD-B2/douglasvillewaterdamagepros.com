Water Damage Services for Winston Homes

The most common Winston calls start with standing water, so water extraction comes first, followed by drying. Storm and creek runoff in the area are handled under flood damage restoration, and supply-line failures under burst pipe water damage.

Winston properties that run on a septic tank or a private well have different risks from homes on public sewer. A backup is handled through sewage backup cleanup, and any long-running moisture is dried with structural drying.

What Winston Residents Should Know

ZIP 30187 spans both Carroll and Douglas counties, so confirm which county your address is in before you plan repairs. Douglas County is the permit authority for Winston addresses inside the county, and a Carroll County address follows that county instead.

Ask whether your home is on public water and sewer through the Douglasville-Douglas County Water and Sewer Authority or on a private well and septic system. The answer decides who to call about a backup, and a leak billing adjustment form from the Authority only matters if you are a customer.

Landmarks help dispatch. Winston Park sits on Bankhead Hwy beside Winston Elementary, Post Road Park is near the interchange, and Clinton Nature Preserve is at 8720 Ephesus Church Rd. Mentioning the nearest one gets a crew to the right side of the community.

Douglas County flood information places the north and east in the Sweetwater Creek floodplain and the southeast in the Chattahoochee floodplain. Check the county map for your parcel, since the answer shapes what your insurance may cover.
