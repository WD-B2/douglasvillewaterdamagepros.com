A Planned Community in East Douglasville

Tributary at New Manchester is a master-planned, mixed-use community of about 1,475 acres in the New Manchester area of east Douglas County, inside the Douglasville city limits. Its design follows New Urbanist ideas, with homes, parks and commercial space close together.

Public records of the area include Founders Park, Riverside Parkway and the Tributary Park of Commerce, where the American Red Cross has a regional headquarters. Public listing records show homes here built from about 2004.

Homes, Townhomes and Commercial Space

A planned community means many building types on the same streets. A single-family leak is handled through burst pipe water damage or water extraction, and a shop or office loss falls under commercial water damage restoration.

Water in attached homes can pass through shared walls and floors. Tell the person answering whether you share a wall or ceiling with a neighbor, and let the association or property manager know early.

What to do if the water came from a neighbor or from outside

Treat water from outside, a drain or a toilet as contaminated, and keep people and pets away from it.

Do not clean sewage-contaminated water with household fans or a standard vacuum, and call (943) 469-0138 to describe what you see.

Near Sweetwater Creek

Sweetwater Creek State Park is next door, and its New Manchester Mill ruins date to a textile mill burned on July 9, 1864. The park became a state park in 1972, according to Georgia sources.

Douglas County says the eastern part of the county is in the Sweetwater Creek floodplain. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status was not verified for Tributary, so check the county map for your parcel rather than assuming either way.

What to Tell Us About a Tributary Address

Say whether you rent, own or manage the unit. The answer changes who needs to be told about the loss, from an owner to a landlord to an association.

Describe the building: single-family house, townhome or commercial unit. Attached and commercial buildings call for different containment and different questions.

Name the street and a landmark such as Founders Park or Riverside Parkway. East Douglasville has several communities, and a landmark places you quickly.

If a leak started in a shared wall or ceiling, photograph both sides if you can reach them safely and keep the photos with the date.

Drying Attached and Commercial Spaces

Containment keeps wet and dry spaces apart, so the drying equipment works on the area that needs it. In an attached building, structural drying follows the water through the wall and floor rather than stopping at a property line.
