Why Is the Floor Warm or the Water Bill Climbing With No Visible Leak?

A pressurized line under a concrete slab can leak for weeks before water shows on the surface. The first signs are quiet ones, such as a warm spot on the floor, a hissing sound at night, a damp baseboard or a water bill that jumps with no change in use.

If the meter keeps turning when every fixture is off, a plumber can confirm a slab leak with leak detection equipment. Once the line is found and repaired, our work begins on the structure that got wet.

If you suspect a slab leak

Check the meter with all water off. If it still moves, close the main shutoff and call a plumber.

Avoid walking barefoot on warm spots, and keep electronics off wet floors.

What a Slab Leak Does to the House

Water under a slab rises through cracks and expansion joints and spreads under flooring. Hardwood cups, laminate swells at the seams, and carpet takes on moisture from below. Wall bases wick water upward, so drywall near the leak often shows stains first.

Plumbers may break the slab to reach a line, or reroute it through the attic. Either path creates dust and moisture, so the dry-out plan follows the repair. The crew measures subfloor moisture, lifts affected flooring and dries the area with air movers and dehumidifiers.

What to Do While You Wait for the Plumber

  • Close the main shutoff and check the meter.
  • Stay off warm floor spots with bare feet.
  • Lift rugs and move electronics off damp floors.
  • Keep leak detection reports and invoices.

Dry-Out Standard After the Repair

Once the line is repaired, drying follows the published ANSI/IICRC S500 approach. Concrete and subfloor are checked against dry reference readings, not the calendar, because a slab can hold moisture long after the surface feels dry.

Insurance and Slab Leaks

Slab leaks are often treated differently from sudden breaks, and policies vary in how they handle the line repair, the access cost and the water damage. Ask your carrier which parts are covered before the plumber breaks the slab, and keep the leak detection report. Photograph the wet flooring and baseboards.

Slab Leaks and the Douglasville Water Meter

The Douglasville-Douglas County Water and Sewer Authority publishes an Application for Leak Billing Adjustment. A slab leak can run for weeks, so the bill is where many owners first notice it, and the form is worth asking about once the leak is repaired and documented.

Where supply lines run in or under a slab, they are hard to reach, so confirm the leak location with detection equipment before any demolition. That one step can turn a large cut into a small one and limits the dust and moisture the repair adds.
