The Villages at Brookmont

The Villages at Brookmont is a planned community in ZIP 30135, west of Bomar Road off Brookhollow Drive. Public listing records show homes built from about 2005 and townhomes added more recently, with a clubhouse, pool, fitness center and tennis and pickleball courts.

Chapel Hills Golf Club at 3300 Golf Ridge Blvd is nearby, and the Brookhollow Drive and Brookmont Pkwy streets are the easiest landmarks when you describe your location.

Single-Family Homes and Attached Townhomes

In a detached home, a leak usually stays in one house, and the job is water extraction followed by drying. In an attached townhome, water can reach a neighbor, so check the unit below or beside you and tell the person answering.

Appliance hoses and supply lines are the typical sources, and the fix starts at appliance leak water damage or kitchen water damage restoration. Wet cabinets and floors are then dried before repairs begin.

What to do if water reached the lower level or a neighbor

Stay out of any lower level where water reaches outlets or the electrical panel, and do not carry electrical equipment into wet areas.

Let the neighbor and the association know quickly, and call (943) 469-0138 to describe where the water traveled.

Drying Before Repairs

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published reference for water loss work, and it treats extraction, moisture mapping and drying as steps that come before repair. Wet cabinets, flooring and drywall are measured, then dried or removed according to what the readings show.

  • Photograph the source and each affected room before anything is moved.
  • Lift small items and furniture legs off wet flooring.
  • Keep receipts for emergency work and any replaced parts.
  • Note the time the water was found and the time it was stopped.

Homeowners Association and Insurance

Many planned communities have rules about repairs, exterior work and common areas. Read your association documents, and ask the manager who handles damage that crosses a unit boundary.

Keep photos, receipts and notes on when the water was found and stopped. Ask your insurer how your policy treats the cause, because coverage depends on the source and the policy terms.

Brookmont Details Worth Mentioning on the Call

Say whether the home is detached or a townhome. That tells the person answering whether to ask about neighbors and shared walls.

Listing records for at least one Brookhollow Drive home describe a full daylight basement, so say whether yours has a lower level. A lower level changes how water spreads and how it is removed.

Give a street such as Brookhollow Drive or Brookmont Pkwy. The community is large, and a street is the fastest way to find the right section.
