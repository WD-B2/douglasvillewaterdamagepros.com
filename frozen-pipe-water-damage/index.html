The Leak Starts When the Ice Thaws

Ice expands inside a pipe and cracks it, but the crack stays plugged while it is frozen. Water starts to flow when temperatures climb and the ice melts, which is why homeowners often find the damage on the first warm afternoon.

That timing matters because the house may be empty or asleep when the plug lets go. The water has often been running for some time by the time anyone notices.

If you suspect a frozen line

Do not use a torch or open flame to thaw a pipe. Use warm air from a hair dryer on a split-free section, and keep the faucet open so melting water has a path.

If you find a split or a wet ceiling, close the main shutoff and call us and a plumber.

Where Frozen Pipes Fail in Georgia Homes

Many North Georgia homes were designed for mild winters, so supply lines often sit in places that are hard to protect. Common weak spots are unheated crawl spaces, attic runs, garage walls and exterior walls with thin insulation. A line on the outside of a wall can freeze in one cold night even when the thermostat is set to normal.

Because these lines are hidden, the first sign is usually water on the ceiling below or a dark stain on a wall. We trace the wet path, extract standing water and open the cavity so it can dry.

Low or no water at a faucet

A freeze may be blocking the line. Open the faucet and leave it dripping while you check for splits.

Frost on exposed pipes

Standards Behind the Dry-Out

After a thaw leak, drying follows the published ANSI/IICRC S500 approach of mapping moisture, setting equipment and checking readings until they match a dry reference. EPA guidance on drying wet materials within 24 to 48 hours explains why a ceiling or wall cavity should not wait for a convenient day.

Insurance and Freeze Damage

Water that escapes after a pipe splits is the kind of sudden loss many policies are written to address, though the policy language on freezing and on heat maintenance varies. Take photos of the split, the stain and any damaged belongings, and keep the plumber invoice. Ask your carrier what it requires for a freeze claim before the repair is complete.

Freeze Events and Douglasville Homes

In a mild-winter climate, owners often skip the foam sleeves and cabinet-door habits that colder states treat as routine. The practical result is that a single hard freeze finds the exposed lines in the crawl space or attic. If you know a freeze is coming, open cabinet doors under sinks on outside walls and let faucets drip.

The Douglasville-Douglas County Water and Sewer Authority publishes an Application for Leak Billing Adjustment. After a thaw leak that ran for hours, ask whether it applies to your account.

Next Steps After a Thaw Leak

Most freeze losses need water extraction followed by drying. If the break was in an unheated space under the house, read about crawl space water damage, and for plumbing failures with no freeze involved, see burst pipe water damage.
