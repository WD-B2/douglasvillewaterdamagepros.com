Extraction and Drying, Step by Step

Extraction removes standing water first, because every hour that water sits it moves into baseboards and subfloor. Moisture meters then show how far the water traveled, and air movers and dehumidifiers dry what can be saved.

Materials that stayed wet too long may need to come out. The scope should say what was removed and why, and ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published reference for how water loss work is done.

Documenting a Loss for Insurance

Take photos of the source and the damage before anything is moved, and keep receipts for any emergency work. Coverage depends on the cause and your policy terms, so ask your insurer which category your loss falls into.
