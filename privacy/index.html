Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it and the choices you have.

This policy describes how Douglasville Water Damage Pros, with a contact address of 3001 Chapel Hill Rd, Douglasville, GA 30135, handles information. Our website has no forms and no accounts. Most information reaches us when you call or email.

Information We Collect

  • Details you share on a call, such as your name, address and a description of the water damage.
  • Information in emails you send us.
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and pages viewed, collected through standard server logs.

Call Recording Notice

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality, training and record-keeping purposes. By continuing a call after hearing or reading this notice, you consent to the recording.

How We Use Information

We use call details to dispatch crews, schedule work, answer questions and keep records of the job. We use email to reply to you. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

We may share job details with the crews, plumbers or other tradespeople working on your property, with your insurance carrier at your request and with service providers who help run our phone and website systems. We may disclose information when the law requires it.

Cookies and Analytics

Our site uses no advertising cookies. If analytics tools are added later, this page will describe them.

Retention and Your Choices

We keep job records for as long as needed for business, tax and legal purposes. You can ask us to correct or delete information we hold about you by emailing contact@douglasvillewaterdamagepros.com, subject to legal requirements.

Children

Our services are directed to adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and Contact

We may update this policy and will post changes here. Questions can be sent to contact@douglasvillewaterdamagepros.com or by calling (943) 469-0138. See also our terms of use.

Questions about privacy?
