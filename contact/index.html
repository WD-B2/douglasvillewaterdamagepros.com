Contact Douglasville Water Damage Pros

The fastest way to reach us is the phone. The line is open 24/7.

Call, Email or Visit

  • Phone: (943) 469-0138, open 24/7.
  • Email: contact@douglasvillewaterdamagepros.com.
  • Address: 3001 Chapel Hill Rd, Douglasville, GA 30135.

Phone calls get the fastest response because the dispatcher can ask questions and start the crew. Email is for non-urgent questions and documents.

What to Have Ready When You Call

  • Your street address and ZIP code.
  • Where the water came from and whether it is stopped.
  • The rooms that are wet and roughly how deep the water is.
  • Whether the water looks clean, dirty or contaminated.
  • Your insurance carrier name, if you have it handy.

When to Call and When to Email

Call for anything involving active water, a spreading stain, a smell that suggests sewage or a safety concern. The line is open at night, on weekends and on holidays, and the dispatcher can start triage while you are still on the phone.

Email works for questions about a past job, requests for copies of moisture readings or general questions about a service. Include your address and a short description so the reply can be specific.

If Water Is Spreading Right Now

Close the main shutoff or the fixture valve first, then call. If water is near outlets, appliances or the electrical panel, keep everyone out of the room and leave the breaker alone until the dispatcher advises you. Photograph the water and the source when it is safe to do so.

The contact address is on Chapel Hill Rd near the Exit 36 interchange on I-20. The service area is Douglasville, Winston and nearby areas. See the service area page to confirm your address, or browse all services.
